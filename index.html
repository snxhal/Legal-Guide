Article 39A: Free Legal Aid for Every Citizen

Did you know? Under Article 39A of the Indian Constitution, the State is required to provide free legal aid to ensure that opportunities for securing justice are not denied to any citizen by reason of economic or other disabilities.

Universal Citizen Rights & Legal Safeguards

Article 22

Arrest & Detention Safeguards

Every citizen has the right to be informed of the grounds of arrest, consult a legal practitioner of choice without delay, and be produced before a magistrate within 24 hours of arrest.

Enforceable Nationwide

Women, Child & Vulnerable Group Protection

Includes special protections like BNSS Sec 46(4) (prohibiting female arrest after sunset without magistrate approval), POCSO Act for minors, and POSH Act for workplace safety.
